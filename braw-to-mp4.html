FAQ

Is HyperRAW free?

Yes. Free mode converts BRAW to MP4 or MOV with UHD output. Every app start includes two successful exports.

Can I convert BRAW to MP4?

Yes—HyperRAW converts Blackmagic RAW (BRAW) to MP4. Free mode renews two successful unwatermarked exports up to UHD at every app start; larger free outputs receive a watermark.

Can I convert BRAW to MOV?

Yes—HyperRAW converts BRAW to MOV. Free mode renews two successful unwatermarked exports up to UHD at every app start; larger free outputs receive a watermark.

Do I need DaVinci Resolve?

No. HyperRAW uses the Blackmagic RAW SDK directly and does not require DaVinci Resolve to be installed. Resolve remains the more appropriate tool when you need native BRAW editing, grading, effects, audio, or finishing. Read the workflow guide.

Does it support 6K/8K/12K/17K BRAW?

HyperRAW can handle high-resolution BRAW sources. Free outputs above UHD receive a watermark; a license enables unwatermarked high-resolution delivery.

When do I need a license?

If you need uninterrupted batches, unwatermarked output above UHD, or advanced workflows, you’ll want a license.
