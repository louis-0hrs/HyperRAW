HyperRAW – FAQ

Quick answers about workflows, performance, hardware support, licensing, and planned features.

← Back to Home Download User Guide (PDF) Contact: HyperRAW.app@gmail.com This page uses no analytics or tracking scripts.

General

What is HyperRAW?
HyperRAW is a standalone, high-performance batch converter for Blackmagic RAW (BRAW) files. It focuses on fast, reliable conversion into common production formats without requiring a full editing suite.
What is a BRAW file?
A .braw file is a Blackmagic RAW camera-media file. It stores encoded image data together with camera and lens metadata used during decoding and post-production. Read the sourced overview: What is a BRAW file?
What’s the typical workflow?
Drag & drop one or multiple .braw files or folders, choose an output codec and settings, then export the ready files in one batch. No timelines, projects, or manual relinking are required.
Do I need DaVinci Resolve installed?
No. HyperRAW uses the Blackmagic RAW SDK directly and works independently of DaVinci Resolve. Resolve remains the more appropriate tool for native BRAW editing, grading, visual effects, audio post-production, and finishing. See how HyperRAW and Resolve fit into the same workflow.
Which operating systems are supported?
HyperRAW is currently available for Windows 10 and Windows 11. macOS and Linux versions are planned; no release date has been announced yet.

Performance & Hardware

Is a GPU required?
No. HyperRAW also runs on CPU-only systems. A compatible GPU can accelerate decoding and processing, especially at higher resolutions, but CPU conversion remains available as a fallback.
Which GPU APIs are supported?
  • CUDA: available for NVIDIA GPUs
  • OpenCL: available for compatible AMD, Intel, and NVIDIA GPUs
  • CPU: available when no suitable GPU is present or selected
HyperRAW detects the available backends and lets you use Auto or select a compatible processing device.
Does HyperRAW support multiple GPUs?
HyperRAW detects available CUDA, OpenCL, and CPU backends. Use Auto for hardware detection or select a compatible backend and device explicitly. Available options and performance depend on your hardware, drivers, and selected export settings.

Formats & Output

Which input formats are supported?
Currently: Blackmagic RAW (.braw).
Which output formats are available?
HyperRAW supports H.264, H.265 (HEVC), AV1, VP9, ProRes-compatible, CineForm-compatible, and FFV1 video output. It also supports JPEG, PNG, TIFF, JPEG-XR, OpenEXR, DPX, and Linear-DNG image sequences, plus WAV audio extraction. The available encoder options depend on your system and installed components.
Does HyperRAW use hardware encoders?
Where applicable, HyperRAW can use system-available encoders. Availability depends on your OS, driver stack, and installed components.

Licensing & Privacy

How much does HyperRAW cost?
Gumroad displays the current license price and acts as merchant of record for the purchase. Gumroad calculates any location-dependent VAT or sales tax and shows the final total before payment. The free Windows mode never expires: every application start includes two successful UHD exports without a watermark.
Does the free mode expire?
No. The free mode has no expiry date and does not require an account. Every time you start HyperRAW, you receive two successful UHD exports without a watermark.
Does HyperRAW upload, watermark, or claim rights to my media?
Processing happens locally. HyperRAW does not upload your footage or claim rights to your input or output files. The free mode produces UHD output without a watermark; larger free exports receive a watermark.
Does HyperRAW require an internet connection?
Online access is required to activate a Gumroad license. During the first weeks after activation, HyperRAW also connects periodically to Gumroad to verify the license key and refund status. Media conversion itself runs locally.
Is there a command-line interface for automation?
Yes. The Windows package includes bin\hyperraw_cli.exe for scripts, scheduled jobs, server-side processing, and agentic media workflows. See the CLI & automation page for examples.
