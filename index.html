What’s new

Current releases for Mac and Windows.

HyperRAW 2026.11.2

macOS · Apple silicon
  • Available for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 13 or later, with Metal decoding and CPU fallback.
  • Native Apple ProRes exports and separately selectable FFmpeg software ProRes.
  • Fixed maximum-width limits in native ProRes exports and combinations of decode scaling and final resizing.
  • Trial watermark eligibility now uses the final output dimensions.

Open the DMG and copy HyperRAW.app to Applications. This build is unsigned and not notarized; Gatekeeper may warn or block it from opening.

  • Encoder test encodes, improved OpenCL detection, and CPU fallback.
